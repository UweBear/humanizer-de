# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. German adaptation of [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) 3.1.0 with 28 patterns in seven sections.

- Kept the structure, workflow, and patterns of the original, with German watch lists and German before/after examples. Instructions stay in English.
- Replaced the dash rule (§9): the spaced en dash is German typography and counts only in clusters; the em dash, spaced or not, is a strong tell.
- Added §8 for connector chains („Darüber hinaus … Zudem … Abschließend“) and §11 for nominal style and light-verb constructions, with an exception for legal and accounting text.
- Added section E, English showing through: §22 for calques and anglicisms, §23 for English typography and spelling. §23 absorbs the original's curly-quote pattern.
- Dropped the original's hyphenated-pairs pattern, which has no German counterpart.
- Added guards from first use on real technical text: a text with few tells gets few edits; plain statements of what the writer did not check are not stacked qualifiers (§10); bold labels that are the content stay (§20); a subtitle that lists what a page covers is weak alone (§27); evidence the reader needs to check a finding is not re-explaining (§28).
- §18 also treats invented titles, page numbers, years, or DOIs as a certain tell.
- §28 keeps the original's reviewer rule and adds the evidence guard.
- Rewrote before/after examples so no "After" adds a fact the "Before" lacks, including several examples carried over from the original.
- Kept the writer's conventions out of scope: *du* or *Sie*, gender-inclusive spelling, spelling variants, technical terms.
